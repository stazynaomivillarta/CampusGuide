ABOUT THE CAMPUS

Rooted in history, driven by innovation.

The University of Science and Technology of Southern Philippines (USTP) was established on August 16, 2016, through Republic Act 10919. This was formed by the amalgamation of the Mindanao University of Science and Technology (MUST) in Cagayan de Oro and the Misamis Oriental State College of Agriculture and Technology (MOSCAT) in Claveria.

The USTP-CDO campus itself traces its roots back to 1927, starting as the Misamis Oriental Trade School. Over the decades, it evolved into the Mindanao School of Arts and Trades, then the Don Mariano Marcos Memorial Polytechnic State College, and eventually Mindanao Polytechnic State College before achieving university status.

Today, it stands as a major campus of the USTP system, strategically located in the heart of Cagayan de Oro City. It is recognized by CHED as a Center of Excellence in Information Technology and a Center of Development in Electrical Engineering and Mathematics.
